THE PEOPLE BEHIND YOUR CELEBRATION

Meet Our Team

Bruna brings the creative vision. Juliana brings every detail together. Together, they create celebrations that feel beautifully, unmistakably yours.

Juliana The Organizing Heart

With an accounting background and a natural love for organization, Juliana is the person who makes sure all the pieces come together. She has a special talent for planning, keeping track of details, and making sure nothing gets overlooked.

If there's something to organize, there's probably an Excel spreadsheet for it — complete with columns, formulas, and maybe even a backup copy!

But beyond the spreadsheets and checklists, Juliana genuinely loves creating a smooth and stress-free experience for couples. She takes care of the details behind the scenes so they can focus on what truly matters: celebrating love and making beautiful memories.

Bruna The Creative Soul

With a background in architecture and a natural eye for design, Bruna is the creative soul behind every celebration. She has a special talent for seeing beauty in the little things and transforming ideas into unique experiences that reflect each couple's personality and story.

From the overall atmosphere to the smallest decorative details, Bruna loves bringing visions to life. She believes that a beautiful celebration isn't just about how things look, but also about how they make people feel.

You can count on her creativity, attention to detail, and probably a few ideas you hadn't even thought of yet!
